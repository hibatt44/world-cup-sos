Calculation notes

Methodology

The dashboard uses live Elo ratings, the confirmed 2026 World Cup draw, and Monte Carlo simulations to compare group paths and tournament probabilities.

Data inputs

Ratings and recent match movement are fetched from eloratings.net. Team names are matched from the Elo team file, and World Cup groups plus knockout slots come from the local tournament structure in this project.

Rest-of-group SoS

For each team, strength of schedule is the average Elo of its three group opponents. The team's own Elo is excluded, which keeps a strong team from making its own path look harder or a weak team from making it look easier.

\[ \mathrm{SoS}_{team} = \operatorname{round}\left(\frac{Elo_{opp1} + Elo_{opp2} + Elo_{opp3}}{3}\right) \]

Group strength

Group difficulty is the rounded average Elo of all four teams in a group. This is useful for comparing whole groups, while rest-of-group SoS is better for comparing each team's specific path.

\[ \mathrm{Group\ Strength} = \operatorname{round}\left(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{4} Elo_i}{4}\right) \]

Match probabilities

Head-to-head win expectancy uses the standard Elo curve. Group matches also include a simplifying draw assumption: draws start at 27% for even teams, then decay smoothly toward 15% as the Elo gap grows.

\[ P(A) = \frac{1}{1 + 10^{(Elo_B - Elo_A)/400}} \]
\[ P(draw) = 0.15 + 0.12e^{-0.004\left|Elo_A - Elo_B\right|} \]

Simulations

The tournament model runs 50,000 bracket-aware simulations. Group matches can end in wins, draws, or losses; knockout matches use the Elo win expectancy directly because every knockout match must produce a winner.

Advancement rules

In each simulation, group tables are sorted by points, then goal difference, then Elo as a tiebreaker proxy. The top two teams in each group advance, followed by the eight best third-place teams across the twelve groups.

Playoff placeholders

If a group contains a playoff placeholder, the model estimates the slot's Elo by weighting each possible playoff winner by its chance to qualify. That expected Elo is then used in SoS and tournament simulations.

What to read carefully

These are model probabilities, not predictions with certainty. They depend on the latest Elo feed, a simplified draw-rate assumption, simplified score generation, and Elo-based tiebreakers where the real tournament would use the full FIFA tiebreaking procedure.
